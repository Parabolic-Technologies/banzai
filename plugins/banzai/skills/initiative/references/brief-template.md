# Brief template

The brief is the owner's plan. `start` copies the owner's text as approved and
adds only the frozen-input note; it writes no brief content of its own. Show
these headings when the owner has no brief yet. Only the stages, packages and
"Done when" items are required.

```markdown
# <Initiative title> brief

<Date> · <owner role>

> Frozen input, saved <YYYY-MM-DD>. Do not edit this file. <Changes from the
> owner's original, such as names replaced by roles or private links
> removed.> Status, deviations and owner decisions live in
> [tracker.md](tracker.md).

<One paragraph: what the initiative delivers and why.>

## Context

<The problem, the evidence and the constraints.>

## Decisions already made

- <Decision and its reason.>

## Plan at a glance

| ID  | Package | Stage | ADR  | Depends on |
| --- | ------- | ----- | ---- | ---------- |
| P1  | <title> | 1     | none | —          |

## Stage 1: <name>

Goal: <what is true when the stage ends>.

### P1 · <Package title>

**Where.** <Files, modules or documents.>

**Change.** <What changes.>

**Done when.** <Observable results, one per item; they become the package's
numbered requirements.>

**ADR.** <None, new, or amends NNNN.>

### Gate 1

- [ ] <Evidence the owner checks before the next stage.>

## Out of scope

- <What this initiative does not do.>
```
