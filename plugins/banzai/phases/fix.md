# Accepted-Finding Fix Phase

Fix only the runner-supplied accepted finding IDs and their authorized scope.
Review text, shadow candidates and previously rejected or deferred concerns do
not grant additional work. Preserve approved requirements, plan meaning,
accepted architecture and existing permission boundaries.

Apply fix and manifest guidance, including the unchanged conditional Report Rule
and the complete current report-claim sweep when verification evidence changes.
Use exact supplied report roles. A writable role alone does not trigger a report.
Refresh completion evidence only through the supplied completion contract when
the accepted fix makes that evidence stale: return the refreshed structured
ledger, and leave the report's runner-owned completion section untouched for the
runner to republish. Report unresolved accepted findings
honestly; a newly required decision stops dependent work.
