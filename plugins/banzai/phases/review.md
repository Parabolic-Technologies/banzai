# Independent Review Phase

Assess the supplied approved scope, current source and frozen evidence in an
independent source-read-only context. Apply the audit guidance and inspect every
required artifact directly, at its exact supplied path. A supplied path or
digest does not prove that its content was assessed.

Use the supplied full or resolution-review scope and authoritative finding
dispositions. Verify accepted findings first in a resolution review; do not
reopen rejected or deferred concerns against unchanged evidence. Report concrete
violations and evidence limitations under the supplied review contract.

Return structured review evidence only. The runner renders, validates and
publishes review artifacts. Do not write a review candidate, canonical review,
source, report or any other file. Freeze the ordinary findings and verdict before
applying the Engineering Quality shadow procedure.
