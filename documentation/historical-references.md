# Historical Work References

Accepted ADRs preserve their original decision text and references. Their links
into `documentation/features/`, `documentation/development-plans/` or
`documentation/changes/` may identify historical workflow evidence omitted from a
clean public snapshot. Those records remain in the owner's private archive under
their original paths and byte identities; public summaries do not replace their
authority. Request authorized access from the project owner when the original
history is needed.

Current product behavior is documented in the root guides, project manifest,
module reference and maintained documentation. Runtime resources, validators and
public tests must use retained public contracts and fixtures. No current public
consumer may require access to an omitted private record.
