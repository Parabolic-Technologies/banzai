# Code of Conduct

Banzai welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Treat participants with respect, give constructive feedback, and
discuss the work without attacking the person. Respect privacy and accept
maintainers' decisions about project scope and participation.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate
intimidation, and publication of someone's private information without
permission are unacceptable. Do not use project spaces for spam or sustained
disruption.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
maintainer-operated community spaces. It also applies when someone represents
the project publicly.

Report concerns privately to
[alexander@parabolic.io](mailto:alexander@parabolic.io). Include enough context to
understand what happened, while avoiding unnecessary personal information.
Maintainers will handle reports as privately as reasonably possible and share
information only as needed to investigate, respond, or meet legal obligations.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may request a correction, remove
content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
They will consider the conduct, impact, and prior behavior. You may request
reconsideration through the same contact address. No response deadline is
promised.

This policy governs participation in community spaces; it does not change the
[software license](LICENSE).
